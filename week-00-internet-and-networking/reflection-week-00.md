## 1. Biggest Technical Insight I Got This Week

The biggest insight was understanding how the internet actually delivers a webpage — it's not magic, it's packet switching. A request from Nigeria to a server in Finland gets broken into small packets, each tagged with an IP address, routed across multiple networks, and reassembled at the destination. TCP/IP ensures nothing is lost along the way, and HTTPS adds encryption so sensitive data stays safe. This demystified what happens in the milliseconds between clicking a link and seeing a page.

## 2. Biggest Insight I Got About Myself This Week

I realised I already understood more networking concepts than I gave myself credit for. Terms like IP addresses, DNS, and protocols sounded intimidating before this week, but breaking them down with real-world analogies (like the phonebook for DNS, or mailing a letter for TCP) made them click instantly. ChatGPT was genuinely useful as a learning companion — asking it to explain concepts in simple terms helped me build confidence.

## 3. My Biggest Challenge

Drawing the architecture diagrams and clearly distinguishing between Two-Tier and Three-Tier applications took more thought than I expected. Explaining packet switching in simple terms without oversimplifying was also tricky. I learned that diagrams force you to truly understand how components connect — you can't fake a diagram.

## 4. One System I Will Implement From This Week

Before every assignment, I will use AI as a learning assistant first — asking it to explain concepts in simple terms before diving into the technical details. This builds a solid mental model that makes the hands-on work much smoother.

## 5. Key Takeaways

- A protocol is like a common language — without agreed rules, devices can't communicate.
- DNS is the internet's phonebook — it translates human-friendly names into machine-readable IP addresses.
- Two-Tier vs Three-Tier architecture shapes how applications scale and how teams organize their work.
- VS Code with an integrated terminal is the foundation for everything coming next.

## 6. My Week 00 Highlight

The highlight was seeing my VS Code environment set up with the terminal running, theme visible, and realising this is where the next 13 weeks of hands-on DevOps work would happen. It felt like the starting line of something real.
